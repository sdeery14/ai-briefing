# ADR 005 — Persistent prerequisite-aware learning

**Status:** Proposed

## Context

Random technical tips do not create cumulative professional development; news-driven learning alone has gaps.

## Decision

Represent tracks, ordered modules and prerequisite-aware lessons independently of LLM-generated wording. Record exposure and explicit feedback, not presumed mastery. News may enrich or occasionally interrupt a thread.

## Consequences

Adds a lightweight curriculum domain; authoring quality and feedback require ongoing human review.

## Revisit when

A specific observed requirement or failure invalidates the current tradeoff. Changes to an accepted decision require a superseding ADR.
